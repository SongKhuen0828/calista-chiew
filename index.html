Holiday residence · Kuah, Langkawi

Where We Belong

A retreat to pause, relax and belong.

Studio
Design Studio 6, final year
Type
Residential, hospitality
Site
Kuah, Langkawi, Kedah
Year
2026

A holiday retreat built as a village rather than a resort. Homes are grouped into small clusters around shared courtyards, linked by shaded walkways and garden corridors. Breaking the massing into smaller volumes keeps the scale human, and porous façades let wind, light and views pass through.

Shops and a café line the road edge to shield the homes from traffic noise. The northeast and southwest winds meet at a retention pond, which acts as a cooling node for the whole site.

Wholesale market redevelopment · Kuala Terengganu

Pasar Borong Gong Pauh

Shifting phases, living market.

Brief
MBKT market upgrade design competition
Type
Commercial, public market
Site
Gong Pauh, Kuala Terengganu
Year
2025

The market is designed around its daily rhythm, from pre-dawn wholesale trading to the stalls that stay open into the afternoon. A modular, cradle-to-cradle timber structure lets bays be reused, extended and rearranged as trade changes over the years.

A planted “green lung” runs through the hall. Timber framing and batik-cloth canopies carry Terengganu's local identity.

Boat-making academy · Pulau Duyong, Terengganu

Tidesmith Atelier

Where timber meets the tide.

Studio
Design Studio 5
Type
Education, cultural
Site
Pulau Duyong, Kuala Terengganu
Focus
Passive ventilation, timber craft

An academy for Pulau Duyong's living tradition of timber boat building. The monsoon and prevailing winds set out a breezeway that forms the building's main spine. Where the two meet, a central courtyard gives people a place to gather and find their way.

The site steps from public to private. Exhibition and F&B spaces face the river, the boat-building dock sits at the semi-private centre, and the academic wing stays quiet at the southern end.

Heritage conservation & adaptive reuse · Kuala Lumpur

Merdeka Morphosis

Evolution towards sustainable modernism.

Result
Top 10 finalist, CIDB × MSSA Open Idea Competition
Type
Conservation, sports venue
Site
Jalan Maharajalela, Kuala Lumpur
Year
2024

A proposal to restore and extend Stadium Merdeka, where Malaysia's independence was declared. A continuous “infinity loop” wraps the existing stadium, linking it with nearby heritage buildings and the new Merdeka 118 tower into one line from past to future.

The loop is a diagrid steel frame with a space-frame truss, double-glazed panels and external GRP solar shading.

Visitor interpretive centre · Hat Yai, Thailand

The Crossroad of Life

Are your choices truly right or wrong?

Studio
Design Studio 4
Type
Community, cultural
Site
Hat Yai Municipal Park, Songkhla
Focus
Narrative, sensory sequence

A centre that turns faith into a spatial experience instead of a lesson. Sacred axes drawn from nearby Buddhist, Hindu and Islamic landmarks lay out the plan.

Visitors move from the Ordinary World through the narrowing, dim Realm of Shadows, then rise into the open City of Light. A water spine guides the way and recalls purification, rebirth and cleansing across the three faiths.

Community pavilion · Hat Yai, Thailand

Trade Through Time

How Hat Yai's night markets came to be.

Studio
Design Studio 4
Type
Pavilion, exhibition
Site
Hat Yai Municipal Park, Songkhla
Focus
Sensory design, local history

A pavilion that tells the story of Hat Yai's markets, from the railway market to the informal, formal and night markets. The form goes from open to enclosed and from daylight to dusk.

Spaces for sound, smell and taste sit on the strongest wind path, so the breeze carries the market atmosphere through the building.
